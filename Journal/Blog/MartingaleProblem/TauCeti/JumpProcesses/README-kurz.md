# Jump processes

A jump process waits an exponential time, jumps according to a kernel, and
repeats. This roadmap constructs it, proves that it solves the martingale problem
of its generator, and develops what that needs: non explosion, the localization
of an unbounded rate, the filtration generated by the jump times, and the path
dependent rate that turns the construction into a Hawkes process.

It is the **worked instance** of the abstract theory of the
**MartingaleProblems** roadmap, which it imports and does not repeat. The
abstract martingale problem, the restart lemma, uniqueness and the Markov
property, the càdlàg modification and the convergence theorems are stated there;
what is here is one class of processes that satisfies them, and the analysis that
class needs.

The original single milestone has been split into six: one construction of this
size is not a milestone but a roadmap.

**What is imported and what is built here.** Several statements this roadmap
reads are general and live in **MartingaleProblems**, not here: the step path
machinery (`IsStepPath`, `measurable_stepIndex_comp`, `measurable_stepPath_comp`,
`eventuallyEq_nhdsGE_stepPath`), joint measurability of a right continuous
process (`measurable_uncurry_min_of_rightContinuous`,
`isStronglyProgressive_of_measurable_uncurry_min`), continuous time martingale
theory (`martingale_stoppedProcess`, `dyadStop`, `stoppedValue_ae_eq_condExp`),
the augmentation of a filtration (`Filtration.augment`, `condExp_augment`,
`Martingale.of_augment`, `naturalFiltration_augment_eq_of_ae_eq`), the almost
sure comparison of natural filtrations (`aeCompletion`, `naturalFiltration_inter_le`,
`martingale_of_ae_eq_of_le_aeCompletion`), the exponential law (`expMeasure_Ioi`),
and the scalar linear equation (`eq_exp_add_integral_of_hasDerivWithinAt`). They
are named below where they are used, and they are **not** milestones of this
roadmap. What is built here is the construction, its non explosion, its
martingale problem, its localization, its examples, and the path dependent rate.

`Suggested.lean` prototypes the signatures. Most of them are discharged there,
against Mathlib `master`; that is evidence the milestones are reachable, not a
prescription of how. Where a proof route is named below it is because the obvious
route is **wrong**, not because it is the one taken.

## Milestone 1: the construction, and non explosion

Data: a measurable rate `lam : E → [0,∞)` and a Markov kernel `mu : Kernel E E`.

* `IsStepPath`, with `IsStepPath.isCadlag`. **A single spike is a step path and
  is not càdlàg**: `1_{\{0\}}` satisfies the naive condition, so the predicate
  must ask for right *local constancy* and not merely for the jump structure.
* `jumpProcess lam mu nu`, and `jumpTimeE`, `jumpProcessE` with jump times in
  `ℝ≥0∞`. **The lift to `ℝ≥0∞` is not bookkeeping**: `a / 0 = ⊤` there is
  already the intended value at a state of vanishing rate, so the construction is
  total without a case distinction, and non explosion becomes a single series
  identity (`jumpTimeE_eq_sum`, `mem_nonExplosiveE_iff_tsum_eq_top`).
* `measurable_stepIndex_comp` and `measurable_stepPath_comp` over an arbitrary
  measurable ordered time axis; `measurable_jumpTimeE`, `measurable_jumpProcessE`.
* Non explosion: the deterministic criteria `mem_nonExplosiveE_of_rate_zero` and
  `…_of_traj`; the almost sure ones `ae_mem_nonExplosiveE`,
  `tendsto_sum_waiting_atTop`; and the **Lyapunov criterion**,
  `ae_mem_nonExplosiveE_jumpMeasure_of_lyapunov` — `A f ≤ C • f` for an `f` with
  exhausting sublevel sets.
* `expMeasure_Ioi` and `expMeasure_Ioi_add` — the exponential tail and its
  memorylessness; `jumpProcess_zero` — the *process* starts with law `nu`, not
  merely the chain that drives it.
* `jumpShift`, `waitingMeasure_map_shift`, `jumpMeasure_map_split` — the restart
  at the first jump, as an identity of measures.
* `jumpMeasure_integral_eq_renewal` — the renewal equation; and the backward
  equation, `abs_integral_jumpProcess_sub_le`,
  `abs_integral_jumpProcess_sub_sub_le`, `jumpMeasure_hasDerivWithinAt_integral`.
  **The derivative is one-sided**, and `eq_zero_of_hasDerivAt_integral_jumpProcess`
  says a two-sided one would be wrong.
* `jumpMeasure_integral_jumpProcess_add` — the Markov property at a fixed time,
  and `…_past` tested against the past.

**Acceptance examples.** The Poisson process as the degenerate case; a two state
chain where the exponential series is a matrix exponential; **explosion**
(`lam n = 2^n`), which is what the unbounded case is about; and a birth and death
chain with its two branches.

## Milestone 2: the martingale problem at a bounded rate

* `lebesgueClock : Clock ℝ≥0`. **The index is `ℝ≥0` and not `ℝ`**, which is what
  the processes carry and what the Skorokhod instances read.
* `jumpApply lam mu f x = lam x * ∫ y, (f y - f x) ∂(mu x)`, the operator, over
  `[MeasurableSpace E]` alone; `jumpOperator lam mu` as the **set of pairs**;
  `norm_apply_le` — for `lam ≤ L` the operator is bounded on bounded measurable
  functions, with constant `2L`.
* `naturalFiltration X hX` — the natural filtration of a measurable process,
  **without a topology on the state space**.
* `eventuallyEq_nhdsGE_stepPath` — the step path is right continuous at every
  time, for **every** sequence of jump times: neither monotonicity nor non
  explosion is needed.
* `measurable_uncurry_min_of_rightContinuous` and
  `isStronglyProgressive_of_measurable_uncurry_min` — a right continuous real
  process is jointly measurable for the past, which is what Mathlib's predicate
  asks and what the compensator needs.
* `jumpProcess_isMPSolution` — **for `lam` bounded, the jump process solves the
  martingale problem for `(A, nu)` with respect to its natural filtration**; and
  `exists_unique_of_bounded`, existence and uniqueness together, through
  Milestone 5 of **MartingaleProblems**.

## Milestone 3: localization, and the unbounded rate

* `jumpFiltrationE` — the natural filtration of the local process.
  `not_isStoppingTime_min_jumpTimeE`: **the jump times are not stopping times for
  it.** `eq_of_measurable_jumpFiltrationE_const_chain` is the sharpening — two
  constant chains are separated by **no** functional of the process, at any time.
* `rateSup`, `rateTime` — the repair, and it is the one Milestone 6 of
  **MartingaleProblems** prescribes: the hitting time of a level by the running
  supremum of the rate along the path. `isLocalizingSequence_rateTime`.
* `truncRate lam n = min lam n`, with `jumpTimeE_le_of_rate_le` (lowering the
  rate delays every jump time, at every sample point and under no hypothesis),
  `jumpProcessE_eq_truncRate_of_le_rateTime`, and
  `jumpProcessE_eq_of_rate_eq_on_path` — **two rates agreeing at every state the
  path has visited give the same process**, with no non explosion hypothesis.
* `naturalFiltration_inter_le` and `jumpFiltrationE_inter_lt_rateTime` — the two
  natural filtrations agree before the hitting time.
* `martingale_stoppedProcess` — the stopped process of a martingale is a
  martingale, **for an index that is not `ℕ`**, through the dyadic approximation
  `dyadStop` of a stopping time **from above** and
  `stoppedValue_ae_eq_condExp`, optional sampling in continuous time in its
  conditional form.
* `jumpProcess_isLocalMPSolution`, and `jumpProcessE_isMPSolution_of_nonneg` —
  the same at a rate **with zeros**, `0 ≤ lam ≤ L`, through `posRate` and
  `ae_absorb_jumpMeasure` (at a state of vanishing rate the chain does not move).
* `aeCompletion` and `martingale_of_ae_eq_of_le_aeCompletion` — the almost sure
  comparison of two natural filtrations.

## Milestone 4: examples, and the master equations

The examples are not decoration: each discharges a hypothesis that no general
statement does.

* The Yule process: `birthDeathRate_yule_apply`, `yuleKernel`,
  `ae_mem_nonExplosiveE_yule`, `jumpApply_yule_indicator` — the generator on an
  indicator, `A 1_{n+1} = β n 1_n - β (n+1) 1_{n+1}`.
* The linear birth and death chain, with `ae_mem_nonExplosiveE_linearBirthDeath`,
  the generating function `hasDerivAt_generatingFunction_linearBirthDeath`, and
  the equation at the absorbing state.
* The M/M/1 queue, `mm1_masterEquation`, the emptiness probe.
* The master equation as a statement: `jumpMeasure_hasDerivWithinAt_integral_Ici`
  and `hasDerivWithinAt_jumpLaw` in differential form;
  `eq_exp_add_integral_of_hasDerivWithinAt`, the scalar linear equation solved by
  the integrating factor; `eq_of_masterEquation` — **an integrated first order
  linear equation determines its solution**; and
  `eq_yuleDensity_of_masterEquation`, uniqueness on the Yule data.
* `tsum_jumpLaw_eq_one` — the one dimensional laws sum to one, **with no
  hypothesis of non explosion**.
* `mem_nonExplosiveE_of_rate_le` — non explosion is **antitone** in the rate.

## Milestone 5: the path dependent rate, and the Hawkes process

The rate at time `t` is a predictable functional of the path rather than a
function of the current state. **This is not a state dependent problem in
disguise**: there is no argument in a state space at which a Lyapunov function
could be applied, so the non explosion criterion of Milestone 1 does not carry,
and the bounded nonlinear case is the one to state first.

* `cumulativeRateF`, `rateInverse`, `jumpTimeF` — the jump times as the inverse
  of the cumulative rate rather than as a quotient.
* The **point filtration**: `pointFiltrationE`, `stepPathFiltrationE`, with
  `stepPathFiltrationE_le_pointFiltrationE` at every index and **under no
  hypothesis**. The converse needs a grid, and two things about it are decided
  rather than guessed: the index `i` must belong to the grid (the rationals do
  not suffice, a jump time landing on `i` being invisible otherwise), and the
  grid is indexed over `Fin (n+1)` because what is needed is a **countable**
  union.
* The **augmentation** by the null sets: `Filtration.augment`, `condExp_augment`,
  `Martingale.augment` and `Martingale.of_augment` — the direction that is needed
  — with `IsStoppingTime.augment`, `IsLocalizingSequence.augment` and
  `Locally.augment`, so that the local property survives.
  `naturalFiltration_augment_eq_of_ae_eq`: two almost surely equal processes have
  the same augmented natural filtration.
* `pointFiltration_augment_eq_stepPathFiltration_augment_of_ae` — **the identity
  of the two filtrations modulo null sets**, with `ae_strictMono_hawkesJumpTime`
  and `ae_move_jumpMeasure_of_ne` discharging its hypotheses on the Hawkes data.
* The bounded nonlinear Hawkes process: `hawkesRate`, `hawkesStepPathH`,
  `hawkesFiltrationH`, and `hawkes_isLocalMPSolution`. **The bounded case needs no
  non explosion**, which is why it is stated first.
* The pieces of the martingale increment, each a statement of its own: the
  exponential formula for a **merely measurable** rate — Mathlib's substitution
  rules do not give it, and the route is absolute continuity of the primitive,
  almost everywhere differentiability, the chain rule and `integral_deriv_eq_sub`;
  the compensator term, a window with two random ends; the jump term, two
  pull-outs with a tower step between them; and Fubini for the conditional
  expectation, whose naive form is not well posed.
* `condExp_chain_mark_block`, `condExp_rate_indicator_block`,
  `condExp_compensator_rate_block` — the conditional survival function of the
  bounded nonlinear process, over the σ-algebra of the jump times.
* The Markov property of the embedded chain at the `n`-th stage, **and that it is
  not an induction**.

**Acceptance examples.** The Hawkes process of the manuscript's `ex:hawkes`; and
the general class it belongs to — **adapted time changes of Poisson processes**,
of which a bounded nonlinear Hawkes process is the thinning.

## Milestone 6: causal convolution and the Volterra resolvent

The renewal equation `m = m₀ + φ ⋆ m` on `[0,∞)` and the resolvent that solves
it. It is the one piece of analysis the jump processes need and do not have: the
path-dependent martingale problem carries `𝔼[N t] < ∞` as a hypothesis, the
manuscript discharges it for the **linear** Hawkes process through this equation,
and the statement that discharges it is the only input missing from an otherwise
complete chain — for the bounded nonlinear process the chain is closed.

**Mathlib has none of it**: `git grep -il` over `master` gives zero hits for
`volterra`, for `renewal` and for `resolvent kernel`. What is there is the
convolution, in two forms, and **the second changes the design of this
milestone**:

* `MeasureTheory.convolution`, Bochner valued, with `ConvolutionExistsAt` as its
  side condition and integrability hypotheses on associativity;
* `MeasureTheory.lconvolution` (`f ⋆ₗ[μ] g`), `ℝ≥0∞` valued and defined by a
  lower integral. Its associativity asks for **measurability and nothing else**,
  and its commutativity asks nothing of the two functions at all — only
  invariance of the measure, which Lebesgue measure on `ℝ` has.

So the milestone is to be built on the `ℝ≥0∞`-valued convolution, with the
causal restriction and the resolvent series, and the passage to the real-valued
statement made once at the end.
